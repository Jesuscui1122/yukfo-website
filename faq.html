FAQ

The questions buyers ask most, answered straight. Anything else, just ask.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

It varies by product and by supplier. Tell me your quantity and I'll confirm the MOQ and price together.

How long does production take?

Lead time depends on the supplier and the process. I don't promise weeks I can't control. A dated schedule is confirmed with every quote, before you commit. Shipping time is quoted with the schedule.

What payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer (T/T): 30% deposit to start production, 70% before shipment. Sample costs are charged separately and deducted from your first production order. Other arrangements are possible for repeat orders.

Do you charge for samples?

Small parts: I'll often cover the sample cost or charge only the cost price. Large parts (molds, rotomolded items) carry a sample/mold fee, which is credited back when you place a production order. The number is shared before we start.

How do I send you my drawings?

STEP, IGES, DXF, DWG, PDF or even a sketch photo, all fine. Send them through the contact form, email, WhatsApp or WeChat, and I'll confirm what I need from you within a day.

Do you make molds and tools?

Yes. I develop injection and rotomolding molds with my partner toolmakers, validate them with trial shots, and only then start production. You'll get mold drawings and a development schedule.

Can you work with my existing molds?

Yes. If you already own tooling, I'll quote production on your molds directly. Many clients transfer their existing molds to our partner factories to reduce cost.

How do you control quality?

Three checkpoints: (1) approved samples before mass production, (2) in-process and final random inspection during production, (3) a pre-shipment inspection report with photos before your goods leave. You can also request third-party inspection.

Can you handle shipping and customs?

Yes. I prepare export documents, handle customs declaration, and arrange sea or air freight to your port or door, per your requirements.

How fast will you reply?

Within 24 hours on working days. Every inquiry gets a direct answer.

Still Have a Question?

Ask it directly. I'll answer personally within 24 hours.
