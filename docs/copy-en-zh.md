| 英文问题 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Who is YUKFO? | YUKFO 是谁？ |
| YUKFO is me, a sourcing partner with six years on the front line of cross-border supply chains. No layers of middlemen: one person pushes the whole project forward, straight to the source. The business runs on word of mouth and clients who come back, so every order matters. | YUKFO 就是我，一个有六年跨境供应链一线经验的采购伙伴。没有层层中间商：一个人把整个项目推到底、直达源头。生意靠口碑和回头客，所以每一单都重要。 |
| Are you a factory? | 你是工厂吗？ |
| No, I don't own factories. I work with partner factories and manage the sourcing process directly for clients, with each supplier picked for the project and checked at each stage. Yukfo Limited is registered in Hong Kong, and the work happens on the ground in the Yangtze River Delta. You deal with one accountable person, not a chain of contacts. | 不是，我不拥有工厂。我通过合作工厂生产，直接为客户管理采购流程，每家供应商按项目挑选并逐阶段核查。Yukfo Limited 注册于香港，实际工作在长三角一线。你对接的是一个负责到底的人，不是一串联系人。 |
| How do you keep pricing transparent? | 报价怎么做到透明？ |
| Every quote is itemized line by line: materials, tooling, processing, finishing, logistics. I work from a database of real cost data across factories and processes, so unreasonable quotes stand out and you pay for what the part actually costs, without middleman markup. | 每份报价逐项列明：材料、模具、加工、表面处理、物流。我基于跨工厂跨工艺的真实成本数据库工作，不合理的报价一眼可见，你为零件实际成本付费，没有中间商加价。 |
| What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)? | 最低起订量是多少？ |
| It varies by product and by supplier. Tell me your quantity and I'll confirm the MOQ and price together. | 因产品和供应商而异。告诉我你的数量，我会连同 MOQ 和价格一起确认。 |
| How long does production take? | 生产需要多久？ |
| It depends on the supplier and the process. Every quote comes with a dated schedule, and the shipping time is quoted right next to it. | 取决于供应商和工艺。每份报价都带具体日期排期，运输时间紧随其后。 |
| What payment methods do you accept? | 接受什么付款方式？ |

Repeat orders can negotiate different terms. | 银行电汇：30% 定金开工，70% 发货前付清。款项进入 Yukfo Limited（香港）。样品费单独收取，从首批量产订单中抵扣。返单可另议条款。 |

| What happens if there is a quality problem? | 出现质量问题怎么办？ |
| Problems are handled where they show up. If a sample fails, it goes back to the supplier with photos and measurements before anything continues. If inspection catches something mid-production, the fix is agreed before the next batch runs. Everything is documented with photos and test results, so there is a record of what was found and what was done about it. | 问题在哪出现就在哪处理。样品不合格，带着照片和测量数据退回供应商，再继续下一步。生产中检查发现问题，先商定修复方案再跑下一批。所有过程用照片和测试结果记录在案，发现什么、做了什么都有据可查。 |
