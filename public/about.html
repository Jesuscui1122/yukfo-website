Why YUKFO Exists

Before founding YUKFO, he spent years on the front line of the supply chain — and saw how projects really run behind the scenes: information out of sync between links, processes that aren't transparent, miscommunication between buyers and factories, and blurry lines of responsibility.

When something goes wrong, some suppliers choose to dodge or hide — and the risk always lands on the overseas buyer: quality nobody follows up on, emails unanswered for days. YUKFO was built to break that pattern: a direct channel to the source, with one person pushing everything forward — no back-and-forth across layers of middlemen.

Expertise

With six years of hands-on cross-border supply chain experience and deliveries across North America, Europe, and Latin America, he knows the production constraints and cost boundaries of multiple processes — plus the practical details like logistics cost calculation. These details decide whether a project lands on time and on budget.

A Foundation of Trust

The business runs on repeat orders and word of mouth — every order directly affects long-term growth. With no communication loss from layered middlemen, collaboration stays transparent and open, and goals always align with the client's: the right factory, a fair cost, and problems identified and resolved early.

Core Advantage

He has built a database of real cost reference data across factories and processes. Every quote is itemized line by line. With first-hand cost data and price comparisons, he can spot unreasonable quotes — so clients pay for what the part actually costs, without unnecessary middleman markup.

Working With Me

No contracts to sign just to ask a question. Send the drawing or idea, and you'll have a response within 24 hours on working days.
