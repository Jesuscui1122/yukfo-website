FAQ

The questions buyers ask most, answered straight. Anything else, just ask.

Who is YUKFO?

YUKFO is me, a sourcing and manufacturing partner with six years on the front line of cross-border supply chains. No layers of middlemen: one person pushes the whole project forward, straight to the source. The business runs on word of mouth and clients who come back, so every order matters.

Are you a factory?

No. All production runs through partner factories, picked for each project and checked at each stage. Yukfo Limited is registered in Hong Kong, and the work happens on the ground in the Yangtze River Delta. You deal with one accountable person, not a chain of contacts.

How do you keep pricing transparent?

Every quote is itemized line by line: materials, tooling, processing, finishing, logistics. I work from a database of real cost data across factories and processes, so unreasonable quotes stand out and you pay for what the part actually costs, without middleman markup.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

It varies by product and by supplier. Tell me your quantity and I'll confirm the MOQ and price together.

How long does production take?

It depends on the supplier and the process. Every quote comes with a dated schedule, and the shipping time is quoted right next to it.

What payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer (T/T): 30% deposit to start production, 70% before shipment. Payments go to Yukfo Limited (Hong Kong). Sample costs are charged separately and deducted from your first production order. Repeat orders can negotiate different terms.

Do you charge for samples?

Small parts: I often cover the sample cost or charge only the cost price. Large parts (molds, rotomolded items) carry a sample/mold fee, credited back when you place a production order. I'll share the number up front.

How do I send you my drawings?

STEP, IGES, DXF, DWG, PDF, or even a sketch photo. Send them through the contact form, email, WhatsApp or WeChat, and I'll confirm what I need within a day.

Do you make molds and tools?

Yes. I develop injection and rotomolding molds with partner toolmakers, validate them with trial shots, then start production. You get mold drawings and a schedule.

Can you work with my existing molds?

Yes. If you already own tooling, I'll quote production on your molds directly. Many clients transfer their existing molds to partner factories to reduce cost.

How do you control quality?

Three checkpoints: (1) samples approved by you before mass production, (2) in-process and final random inspection, (3) a pre-shipment report with photos. Third-party inspection available.

What happens if there is a quality problem?

Problems are handled where they show up. If a sample fails, it goes back to the supplier with photos and measurements before anything continues. If inspection catches something mid-production, the fix is agreed before the next batch runs. Everything is documented with photos and test results, so there is a record of what was found and what was done about it.

Can you handle shipping and customs?

Yes. Export documents, customs, sea or air freight to your port or door. I handle it.

How fast will you reply?

Within 24 hours on working days. Every inquiry gets a direct answer.

Still Have a Question?

Ask it directly. I'll answer personally within 24 hours.
