Why a One-Person Partner?

I live on repeat orders and referrals. That means the right factory, a fair cost, and clear expectations on every order.

Finding a factory is the easy part. The work is in comparing quotes, checking samples, chasing schedules, and catching problems before they ship. That work is what a sourcing agent is for: one contact who answers for the whole chain, with itemized quotes so you see the cost of each step.
